## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-performance, avant-garde streetwear aesthetic rooted in modern algorithmic culture and industrial luxury. Tailored for digitally native collectors, performance enthusiasts, and tech-forward fashion consumers, the interface projects technical precision, exclusivity, and dynamic velocity.

Visually, the style merges **High-Contrast Brutalism** with **Engineered Glassmorphism**:
- Obsidian surfaces provide infinite optical depth, letting tactile product renders and data-dense overlays dominate the viewport.
- Razor-sharp structural lines, mechanical micro-grids, and monospaced telemetry data evoke speculative military ergonomics, aeronautics, and encrypted physical assets.
- Micro-interactions are immediate and stiff, reflecting instant biometric response rather than fluid leisure.

## Colors
The palette leverages extreme value contrast anchored by deep obsidian foundations and targeted spectral strikes.

- **Primary (`#10B981` Hyper-Lime):** Serves as the primary operational signal. Used exclusively for primary purchase triggers, authentication milestones, and confirmed transactional states.
- **Secondary (`#00F0FF` Laser Cyan):** Applied to telemetry data displays, micro-grid markers, biometric readouts, and interactive cursor anchors.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` Warning Amber):** Communicates drop urgency, stock scarcity, release countdowns, and critical biometric mismatch warnings.
- **Neutrals:**
  - Base canvas is `#09090B` (Deep Obsidian).
  - Raised structural surfaces use `#111113` (Matte Carbon).
  - Structural borders use `#27272A` with selective semi-transparency.
  - High-visibility text and crisp graphical focal points utilize `#FAFAFA` (Bone White).
  - De-emphasized data labels and secondary details utilize `#71717A` (Muted Zinc).

Avoid diffuse color floods. Color must function strictly as diagnostic information and structural focus.

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy relies on a calibrated tension between editorial expression and structural computing:

1. **Display & Headlines (`Syne`):** High-impact, broad geometric character used for high-fashion titling, product code names, and drop launch headers. All display styling defaults to uppercase or tight-kerned title case.
2. **Body (`Inter`):** Neutral, hyper-legible grotesque sans-serif handling narrative descriptions, specifications, checkout sequences, and customer reviews without adding friction.
3. **Telemetry & Badges (`JetBrains Mono`):** Fixed-width telemetry typeface deployed for dynamic pricing, drop dates, inventory allocation IDs, sizing chips, and data-density overlays. Always uppercase with positive tracking.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout employs an asymmetric, editorial 12-column fluid grid on desktop (8-column on tablet, 4-column on mobile). 

- **Density & Contrast:** High structural density alternates with dramatic negative space. Full-bleed editorial silhouettes contrast against tight, information-rich diagnostic panels.
- **Micro-Grids & Coordinates:** Sections are demarcated by visible 1px hairline rules (`#27272A`), corner tick-marks, and axis indices to emphasize calibrated construction.
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Asymmetrical lockups with sticky product telemetry sidebars flanking sprawling media feeds.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Balanced two-column modular arrangements with persistent top-level drop monitors.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked cards with bottom-anchored transactional panels, horizontal swipe feeds for variant exploration, and compact telemetry headers.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation eschews diffuse, organic shadows in favor of sharp spatial layering and translucent surface stacking:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure Obsidian (`#09090B`).
  - **Level 1 (Card/Container):** Carbon (`#111113`) enclosed by a 1px boundary (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
  - **Level 2 (Floating Drawer / Nav Dock):** Glassmorphic obsidian substrate (`rgba(9, 9, 11, 0.75)`) backed by a 16px blur filter and a 1px top highlight (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Telemetry Glow:** Critical interaction states feature a tight, concentrated peripheral aura: `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` for active inputs or completed checkout triggers.

## Shapes
Geometry is strictly **Sharp (0px radius)** across all modules, framing, and interactive surfaces.

- Buttons, form elements, modal trays, badges, and image frames maintain orthogonal 90-degree corners.
- Angled 45-degree chamfers (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) may be used selectively on high-tier metadata tags, edition badges, and quick-add action triggers to reinforce technical apparel construction.
- Avoid soft, friendly curves anywhere in the design hierarchy.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Acquire / Drop Access):** Solid Bone White (`#FAFAFA`) background with pure Obsidian text (`#09090B`) in bold `JetBrains Mono`. 0px border radius, sharp hover state that transitions instantaneously to Hyper-Lime (`#10B981`) with negative cursor pull.
- **Secondary (Telemetry Spec / Waitlist):** Transparent background, 1px perimeter border in `#27272A`, text in Bone White. Hover activates a faint glass overlay (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) and Laser Cyan border illumination (`#00F0FF`).
- **Destructive / Error:** Sharp fill in `#F59E0B` with dark glyphs for critical supply drops or payment errors.

### Chips & Badges
- Constructed with monospaced typography (`label-sm`), 0px border radius, and tight padding (`0.25rem 0.5rem`).
- Bordered with 1px hair lines. Status indicators (e.g., `VERIFIED SENSOR`, `ALLOCATED`, `LOW STOCK`) incorporate a live blinking 4px square LED node colored in Hyper-Lime or Warning Amber.

### Form Inputs
- Background is set to Carbon (`#111113`) with a 1px default border (`#27272A`).
- Inner text uses `JetBrains Mono` for inputs (sizes, authentication tokens, delivery addresses).
- Focus states trigger an instantaneous `#00F0FF` border snap with zero animation damping, accompanied by a small corner coordinate flag.

### Checkboxes & Selectors
- Rigid square geometries with 0px rounding.
- Active state renders a solid Hyper-Lime (`#10B981`) inner square with sharp cross-cut telemetry markings rather than rounded checkmarks.

### Product Cards & Modals
- Modular container system with hairline borders. Images feature high-contrast editorial photography treated with neutral grading against deep obsidian backdrops.
- Hover reveals technical schematics: shoe weight, algorithmic authentication signature, and real-time bid/value curves superimposed via semi-transparent data HUD overlays.
